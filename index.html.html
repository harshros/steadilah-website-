SteadiLAH

A simple, human-centred solution designed to help people stay steady, confident and supported in their everyday lives.

What is Steadilah?

Steadilah is a compact, 2-piece intelligent wearable engineered to stabilize hand tremors through real-time reactive neuro-electric stimulation. By pairing 9-axis motion tracking and EMG muscle sensing with adaptive frequency modulation, Steadilah provides dynamic, all-day stability without bulky mechanical parts, keeping you seamless, steady, and connected.
Why Steadilah?

Intelligent stability, designed around you.

Closed-Loop Intelligence

Built-in IMU and EMG sensors constantly process hand signals to differentiate intentional movement from involuntary tremors, firing pulses only when needed to prevent habituation and fatigue.

Reactive Neuro-Electric Pulses

Delivers dynamic electrical stimulation targeting key nerve pathways to calm tremors right at the source.

Lightweight 2-Piece Ergonomics

A streamlined glove and elbow piece designed for soft, waterproof, continuous daily wear offering an all-electronic alternative to heavy spinning gyroscopes.

Connected Health Tracking

Real-time data syncs seamlessly to your phone or personal dashboard, letting you track tremor intensity and management progress over time.
